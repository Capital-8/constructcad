# Architecture: Sketches, roofs, masses, site elements, shafts, sweeps

Part of the architecture notes indexed in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Keep each topic in its bullet here; AGENTS.md only lists the topics.

- **Sketches** — `cc_core::sketch`: a `Sketch` is the `SketchCurve`s (lines, bulge arcs, circles, whole or partial ellipses, splines: `SketchCurve::Spline` holds the control points of a chain of cubic Béziers, `cc_core::spline` interpolates the points clicked and splits, offsets and measures them exactly, and component profile points carry them as `ProfilePoint::bezier` handles, `profile::Segment::Bezier`) an outline was drawn with; `Sketch::profile` chains them into loops (ends within `JOIN_TOL`, bounded, arcs and ellipses split every `ARC_STEP`) and returns the outline and its openings, or a `SketchError` with the point (open end, branch, crossing, apart, nested, openings where none are allowed). Floors, ceilings, roofs and masses keep theirs in an optional `sketch` field (`Element::drawn_sketch`, `sketched_profile`); `Element::transform`/`translate` move it, and `EditCtx::finish` (`sync_sketches`) and `repair` drop one that no longer makes the outline (`Sketch::makes`), so editing the points directly leaves the curves behind. A floor's area (`Floor::area`) and volume (`ModelGeometry::curved_volumes`) follow the true curves of a curved sketch (`Sketch::area`, `SketchCurve::swept_area`), not the chords of its outline; IFC import keeps the circles and arcs of a slab's profile (`IfcCircleProfileDef`, trimmed circles and arc segments in `IfcArbitraryClosedProfileDef`, `Geometry::plan_curves`) as the floor's sketch, and export writes a curved sketch's circles and arcs as such (`Sketch::curve_profile` gives each loop's curves in order, #368). `cc_engine::commands::sketch`: `CurveArg` (also rectangles and polygons, as lines) is the `curves` argument of `floor_create`, `ceiling_create`, `roof_create`, `foundation_slab_create` (outline only, not kept) and `mass_create`; `sketch_get` and `sketch_set` read and redraw an outline. In the app, `sketch_mode` holds the open sketch (`AppState::sketch`, opened by `set_tool` with a `Tool::Sketch`, or by *Edit Boundary*): the main ribbon shows it (`ribbon::sketching`; the component environment keeps `sketch_mode::ribbon`) as the contextual Modify tab, `ribbon::follow_sketch` bringing that tab forward when a sketch opens and the tab before back when it closes (`AppState::ribbon_sketching`), the other tabs disabled meanwhile; `sketch_mode::title` names it (*Modify | Create Boundary: Floor*) and `sketch_mode::groups` draws Select, Modify, Draw (with `SketchPickWalls` and `SketchPoint` when the sketch takes them) and Mode (*Finish*/*Cancel*, tinted green and red by `ribbon::big_button_ink`), the canvas (preview, snapping to the sketch's points, picking, grips that move joined ends together), the picked curve in the properties palette and its own undo, window and chain selection, and the Modify tools (move, copy, rotate, mirror, offset, trim/extend, split: the same `UiCommand`s, routed to the sketch while one is open, the geometry in `cc_core::sketch_edit`); *Finish* runs the creation command with `curves` or `sketch_set`. The component environment's pieces are sketched in the same session (`Target::Piece`, `Session::piece`; `component_sketch` snaps to its reference planes and writes the curves as profile points, locked to planes, keeping the expressions of points that did not move when a piece's sketch is edited). *From Walls* (`sketch_mode::pick_walls`, `cc_engine::walls_loop_at` on the options bar's `ModifyOptions::wall_face`) adds lines with `Session::add_from_walls`, which keeps each pass in `Session::from_walls` (in the undo snapshots); `set_wall_face` recomputes the loop on the new face and `Session::refit_walls` moves, side for side, the lines still as they were made (#496).
- **Roof coverings and framing** — `cc_core::roofing`: `TypeData::Roof { covering, framing }` (serde defaults): a `TileKind` from the built-in catalog (`TileKind::spec`: least slope, weight, tiles per m², batten gauge, purlin span) and a `RoofFraming` (system None/Timber/TimberTrusses/Steel, material, purlin/rafter/batten `MemberSize`s and spacings, repaired on load). `cc_geom::roof_framing` frames each sloped underside plane of the roof's built solid (so it follows `Roof::edge_overhangs`, see geometry.md), coplanar pieces together (battens at the gauge, rafters down the slope, purlins spread evenly; trusses: also king-post trusses across the eave direction of the largest face, cut from the faces sloping across them moved under the purlins, chords met at the apex, chords apart making separate trusses; steel: purlins only), as boxes across the roof plane; `build_slabs` stores it in `ModelGeometry::roof_framing` and adds the members as a `Category::Beam` solid of the roof element marked `Solid::secondary`: drawn, but left out of `ModelGeometry::body_of` (volume, joins, plan edges, IFC body), and section cuts hatch it in the framing's material. The covering's rules (`TileSpec::accepts`, `tiles`, `weight_on`) are shared by the check, properties and schedules; `type_set` gives a new covering the framing it usually lies on when none was chosen. `roof_check` and `element_get`'s `roofing` give the slope against the covering's least, tiles, weight and framing figures; schedules have `TileCount`, `FramingLength`, `FramingVolume`, and `Slope`/`Weight` for roofs. IFC export writes each member as an `IfcMember` (kind from `MemberKind` and `Member::truss`) aggregated in the `IfcRoof`, typed by an `IfcMemberType` per roof type and member name, and the type's choices in `ROOFING_PSET` (`ConstructCAD_Roofing`); import restores them on the roof's type (`Importer::roofing`) and leaves out the members aggregated in a roof so framed (`framed_roofs`).
- **Roof joins** — `cc_core::Roof::join_to` (serde default, format 34) is the only stored data: the roof a cross wing or bay is joined to. `Project::roof_join_chain`/`roof_join_loops`/`roof_join_group` walk the joins (bounded, visited sets) and `broken_roof_joins` finds those that must go (to a missing element, to itself, to what is no roof, and the highest id on each loop): `EditCtx::finish` (`sync_attachments`) clears them, so deleting the main roof frees its wings in the same undo step, and `repair` clears them on load; `paste_elements` points a copied wing at the copy of its roof when both were copied, else keeps its roof. `cc_geom::roof_join::resolve` runs at the start of `build_slabs` (nothing to do without a join): each roof's slab is a set of `Piece`s (a plan ring, counter-clockwise, holes joined by keyhole bridges, and its underside `z = c + g · p`) from `roofs::body` (`RoofBody::pieces`: the skeleton's faces and flats, the fallback's regions, a shed's plane, a prism), with its vertical thickness. The wing's meeting run is its longest run of footprint edges whose middles lie on the target's pieces (within 1e-6), else, when its outline does not overlap the target, the edge whose middle looks out at the target nearest, within the wing's diagonal (the gap); none is `Apart`. When both edges beside the run are sloped eaves of a gable or hip wing (a cross wing's end), the run is replaced by one gable edge (`Some(0.0)` slope and overhang) moved past the target along those edges (shorter, else the run moved square, when that crosses them; not simple: `Failed`); a wing whose run lies between gables is only cut. Of each piece of the wing as built, it keeps the part outside the target within its own outline (`roofs::outline` with the run moved across the gap) and, over each target piece, the part where its top is above the target's (`roofs::clip_half_plane`, `region::covered`/`covered_except`, `MAX_JOIN_POINTS`); of those, the parts sharing edges with the part outside (or, with none, with its own outline), which drops patches coming up past the target's ridge. Valleys are the kept pieces' boundary edges over the target where both tops meet (1e-6); none is `Apart`. The target is cut under the kept pieces (`cut`); ridges and hips are clipped to what each keeps and the valleys added (`roof_ridges`). Chains resolve from the free end (each roof after its own join is the target of the next), several wings meet their roof uncut, loops are `Cycle` and missing targets (phases, design options) `Missing`: only `Joined` changes the roofs. `add_pieces` builds the closed slab (tops, undersides, and a vertical face along every part of an edge no other edge runs back along, which cancels shared edges, bridges and T-junctions: volume = plan area × vertical thickness). `ModelGeometry::roof_joins` holds a `RoofJoinGeom` per roof joined or cut (`to`, `state`, `cut_by`, `outline_edges` without the valleys, `valleys` on the tops, `area` within the footprints of it and its partners, `plan_area`, `sloped_area`); `preview` resolves one join before it is made. Unjoined roofs build as before. Consumers: framing, tiles, slopes, volume, sections and 3D read the solid; `attach_wall_tops` follows every roof of the built join group (`joined_group`); plans draw an active roof's `outline_edges` and its ridges with the valleys; the schedules' Area is `RoofJoinGeom::area`; `element_get` gives `plan_area` and `derived.join` (`to`, `active`, `issue`, `valleys`, `valley_length`, `cut_by`); `roof_check` reports a join that is not built (`msg-roof-join-inactive`) and gives `join`; IFC see interop.md. `cc_engine::commands::roof_join`: `roof_join` (both roofs, distinct, no loop, and the preview must be `Joined`) and `roof_unjoin`; `element_set` of `join_to` is checked the same way except the preview (one that does not meet stays, inactive). Not handled: wings that overlap each other, shafts cutting the main roof under a wing, dormers. App: see *Roof joins* in app.md. 
- **Conceptual masses** — `cc_core::mass`: `ElementKind::Mass(Mass)` (category `Mass`, a `TypeData::Mass { material }` type created with the first mass) is a base `outline` extruded `height` up from its level plus `base_offset`, or lofted to a `top` outline (`cc_core::sweep::blend`; outlines with as many points join vertex to vertex, so an inset is exact), with `floors`, the levels where mass floors are taken. `cc_geom::mass` builds the see-through body and the mass floors (`Mass::section` at each listed level the mass spans, with area and perimeter; `ModelGeometry::masses`); plans draw a mass where it stands or is cut (`draw_mesh`) with the floor of their level and its area; schedules have `FloorArea` and `FloorCount`. `cc_engine::commands::mass` holds `mass_create` (with `top` or `top_inset` for a loft), `mass_floors_set`, `mass_floors` and `mass_type_create`; deleting a level drops its mass floors. `mass_walls_create`, `mass_slabs_create` and `mass_roof_create` build walls (exterior face on the mass, `LocationLine::FinishExterior`, outline run clockwise), floors on the mass floors and a flat roof from a mass. IFC export leaves masses out, like imported drawings.
- **Terrain** — `cc_core::topography`: `ElementKind::Topography(Topography)` (category `Topography`, a `TypeData::Topography { material, contour_interval, major_every }` type created with the first terrain, in the earth material the template carries or `topography_create` adds) holds measured `points` (z above the level), a closed `boundary` (with an optional sketch) and `base_depth`; repair drops bad and repeated points, and its defaults and limits are the module's constants (`DEFAULT_*`, `MIN_CONTOUR_INTERVAL`, `EARTH_COLOR`, `earth_material`). `cc_geom::topography` triangulates the points inside the boundary with its edges as constraints (`spade`, constrained Delaunay, inside `catch_unwind`), gives the boundary the elevations all the points imply (barycentric, else the nearest point), closes the surface down to a flat base into a solid (`ModelGeometry::topographies`, analytic volume) and cuts `contours`; `cc_views::topography` draws plans (contours below the cut, the earth above it hatched). `cc_engine::commands::topography` holds `topography_create`, `topography_points_set` and `topography_type_create`; IFC export writes an `IfcGeographicElement` `TERRAIN` with `TOPOGRAPHY_PSET` (`ConstructCAD_Topography`), which import reads back. `sketch_set` takes a terrain's `points` with its curves (one step). In the app the sketch takes points (`Draw::Point` at `ModifyOptions::point_elevation`, `sketch_mode::Session::points`, picked and deleted with the curves).
- **Building pads** — `cc_core::pad`: `ElementKind::Pad(Pad)` (category `Pad`, a `TypeData::Pad { cut_slope, fill_slope }` type created with the first pad; ratios are run per unit of rise, kept in `MIN_SLOPE..=MAX_SLOPE`) is a flat `outline` at `elevation` above its `host` terrain's level, with optional own slopes. Repair drops a pad whose host is not a terrain; `EditCtx::finish` → `sync_pads` keeps its level the host's; it is deleted (`remove_element`), transformed and copied with its terrain (`Project::pads_of`). `cc_geom::pad::grade` applies each pad of a terrain in id order to `pad::Surface` (`topo_geom_with_pads`): `F = max(L, min(T, C))` over the triangles within reach of the pad, re-triangulated with spade with their edges as constraints (split where the outline and daylight lines cross them; new points settled off slivers, `Index::settle`), so cut and fill integrate exactly; `TopoGeom::existing` keeps the original ground where pads changed it, `ModelGeometry::pads` the `PadGeom`s (volumes, daylight lines, slope rays, warnings). `cc_views::topography` draws the pad (outline, elevation, slope ticks, daylight, the existing contours dashed) and sections dash the existing ground line (`projected::existing_ground`). `cc_engine::commands::pad` holds `pad_create` (`default_host`) and `pad_type_create`; IFC export writes a terrain's pads in `PADS_PSET` (`ConstructCAD_Pads`), import recreates them. In the app: *Massing & Site → Site → Building Pad* (`ModifyOptions::pad_elevation`, available when the plan's level has a terrain).
- **Shafts** — `cc_core::shaft`: `ElementKind::Shaft(Shaft)` (category `Shaft`, a `TypeData::Shaft {}` type created with the first shaft) is an `outline` from its level plus `base_offset` to `top` (a `WallTop`) or `height`, with an optional sketch. Floors, ceilings and roofs keep the shafts' outlines in `cuts` (derived: `EditCtx::finish` → `sync_shafts` writes `Project::shaft_cuts`, only where they changed; `shaft::fits` requires the outline inside the slab and clear of its openings; repair drops bad ones). Readers chain them with the element's own openings (`Floor::all_openings`, roof holes without the overhang shrink, ceilings' holes); plans draw the shaft as an outline with two diagonals. `cc_engine::commands::shaft` holds `shaft_create` and `shaft_type_create`.
- **Swept profiles** — `cc_core::profile_sweep`: `ElementKind::Sweep(Sweep)` (category `Sweep`) is a `path` in plan (open, or `closed` around a room) at `base_offset` above its level, with `flip` putting the profile on the right; its `TypeData::Sweep { profile, material }` type holds the profile (x across, to the left of the path; y up), checked by `check_profile` (closed, not crossing, bounded) and reset to `baseboard()` by repair. `cc_core::sweep::sweep`/`sweep_closed` miter it along the path, `cc_geom::profile_sweep::sweep_faces` places it, plans draw it with `draw_mesh`, legends its section, IFC export an `IfcCovering` `MOLDING`. A path may have arcs (#366): `Sweep::bulges` (serde default, skipped when empty; no format bump) holds the bulge of each segment (`bulges[i]` from point `i` to the next, the closing one's kept unused while the path is open; missing ones straight), so a circle is a closed path of two points and two half circles; `segments`, `length` (arcs as arcs), `stations` (bounded by `MAX_STATIONS`), `is_valid` (two points close around an arc) and `repair` (before the element loop: at most one per point, numbers, `MAX_BULGE`); `Element::transform` negates them under a mirror. `path_from_curves` chains lines, arcs and one circle (`PathProblem`: unsupported curves, branches where the path would touch itself, curves apart, a retraced loop) into points and bulges, running the way the first curve runs. `cc_geom::profile_sweep::sweep_mesh` (faces and `smooth` flags) builds a curved path's solid itself (`curved_mesh`): a section at every station, radial and unscaled on the arcs (`segments(sweep, ARC_STEP)`), mitered between true tangents at corners, sides split in triangles where not flat, the facets of the profile's non-level edges on arcs (and of its own arcs when the type's sketch is curved, `section::smooth_sides`) smooth; `exact_volume` (Pappus: area times the centroid's path, arcs at the centroid's radius, `2 c tan(θ/2)` off at each mitered corner; none past `MAX_MITER`) goes into `ModelGeometry::curved_volumes`. Plans pass the smooth flags to `mesh_piece`; IFC export stays chorded. `cc_engine::commands::sweep` holds `sweep_create` (`path` points or `curves`, as `floor_create` takes them; a project without a sweep type gets a baseboard), `sweep_type_create` and `sweep_profile_set` (points or sketch `curves`).
