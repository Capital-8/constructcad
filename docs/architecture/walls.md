# Architecture: Walls: curtain walls, profiles, splines, niches, flip

Part of the architecture notes indexed in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Keep each topic in its bullet here; AGENTS.md only lists the topics.

- **Curtain walls** — `cc_core::curtain`: a wall type whose `TypeData::Wall::curtain` holds a `CurtainSystem` (vertical and horizontal `CurtainGrid`s: layout, spacing, count, justification; the default `CurtainPanel`: glass, solid, empty or a component type; interior and border `Mullion`s) has no layers (`ElementType::layers` is empty, `thickness` is the deepest mullion or panel). Each `Wall` keeps `curtain: CurtainEdits` (its own lines, removed `GridSegment`s and `PanelSwap`s, by position: `u` along the body centerline, heights above the base; `reverse` under a mirror), and `curtain::layout` works out the grid (cells merged across removed segments, panels between the mullions, mullion pieces), bounded by `MAX_LINES`. `cc_geom::curtain` places it over the wall's inner range (flat panels on the chords of an arc wall, component panels resolved with `Width`/`Height` and `WallThickness`) in `WallGeom::curtain`, with solids per material; a straight curtain wall inside a parallel straight wall (`wall::embedded_in`) joins nothing and opens its host like a door. Plans draw mullions and panel lines (`plan::draw_curtain`); schedules have `PanelCount`, `GlazedArea`, `MullionLength`. Commands in `cc_engine::commands::curtain` (`curtain_type_create`, `curtain_get`, `curtain_grid_set`, `curtain_panel_set`). IFC export writes an `IfcCurtainWall` (axis only) aggregating an `IfcPlate` per panel and an `IfcMember` (`MULLION`) per mullion piece, typed per curtain type and kind, and the grid in `CURTAIN_PSET` (`ConstructCAD_Curtain`: `System` and `Edits` as `curtain::to_text`, `Height`, `Bulge`); an embedded curtain wall's host only gets an opening. Import rebuilds from that set, else from the parts when they make a plain straight grid (`Importer::curtain_from_parts`: one plate per cell, mullions sized by the members, the lines kept as the wall's own); otherwise the parts come in one by one. Component panels come back as the type's glass. In the app (`curtain_ui.rs`): the wall tool's type list makes a curtain type when the project has none (`new_curtain_type`), the type's grid, panel and mullion sections in Type Properties, the grid rows of a selected wall in the palette, and the Curtain Grid tool (`Tool::CurtainGrid`, options in `AppState::curtain`), which picks a wall and a point on it in plans (at the cut height) and in elevations and sections (through `View::projection`) and runs `curtain_grid_set` or `curtain_panel_set`.
- **Wall profiles** — `cc_core::wall_profile`: `Wall::profile` (`Option<WallProfile>`, format 31; 30 belongs to another change) is the outline of the wall's face in its elevation frame (`u` along the drawn location line from its start, the same `u` as doors and the body centerline of a straight wall; `z` above `Project::wall_base_z`), CCW `outline` points plus an optional `Sketch` of the curves it was drawn with (dropped when it no longer makes the outline, `drop_stale_sketch`). It is one simple loop, monotone along the wall (`Chains`: a `bottom` and a `top` chain by increasing `u`, vertical ends and steps allowed; `ProfileError` with the point and an i18n `key` otherwise), no holes, within `[0, length]` and `MAX_BELOW`/`MAX_ABOVE`; `holds` tests a door's rectangle, `area` is exact along the sketch's curves, `reversed` mirrors it, and `refit(old_length, shift, new_length)` keeps it where it was along the wall, cuts it at the ends (curves cut on their own geometry, else the outline clipped and the sketch dropped) and moves the points of an end it reached when the wall grows there. Only straight walls that are not curtain walls take one: `EditCtx::finish` → `sync_wall_profiles` refits it when a wall's length changes (a start moved along the wall's own line shifts it; a plain move or turn leaves it) and drops it when the wall becomes an arc or a curtain wall; `Element::transform` reverses it under a mirror and `wall_ops::split` gives the second part its share; `repair` drops a bad one, cuts one past the end and drops `attach_top` of a profiled wall (the profile wins). The height, top constraint and `top_z` stay the nominal box. `cc_geom`: `build_walls` lifts the chains to absolute heights; an end the profile stops short of (`END_REACH`) joins nothing and is capped square at the profile's end (`start_ext`/`end_ext`); the walls' height overlap for joins uses the profile's range; openings and niches not `holds` inside it are left out (not cut or drawn); `WallGeom::set_elevation` (after `set_openings`) splits the columns at the chains' points and stores `bottom_profile`/`top_profile` segments and `profile` (`is_profiled`); `intervals_at` takes the lowest interval's bottom and the highest's top from the chains where they are not an opening's; `to_solid` slopes the base faces and the sides (`face_pieces` with a bottom line), `volume` evaluates the heights at each column footprint's centroid (exact for planes); `cut_columns(z)` splits columns where the chains cross a level plane (plans draw a profiled wall cut only there, seen below or not drawn above per the usual column states), `solid_at(u, z)`, and `profile_outline` (the outline carried on to mitered ends' corners). Wall bars use them (horizontal bars stop where the body ends at their height, vertical bars between the chains with the cover at the base); `attach_wall_tops` skips profiled walls. Schedules' wall `Area` is the profile's less the doors inside it. Commands in `cc_engine::commands::wall_profile`: `wall_profile_set` (`curves` or `points`), `wall_profile_slope` (`top_start`, `top_end`, `base_start`, `base_end`), `wall_profile_remove`; `sketch_get`/`sketch_set` take walls (`frame: "wall_elevation"`, the nominal rectangle with `profile: false` when none), `wall_create` takes `profile` curves, `element_get` `derived.profile` (`area`, `min_z`, `max_z`, `start`, `end`); arc, curtain, attached and framed walls are refused, `wall_attach` and `wall_framing_create` refuse profiled walls. IFC export sweeps the outline across the wall (`IfcExtrudedAreaSolid` in the axis plane, clipped by `IfcHalfSpaceSolid`s at mitered ends, `Exporter::clip_end`, representation `Clipping`), quantities from the profile, and `WALL_PROFILE_PSET` (`ConstructCAD_WallProfile`: `Profile` as `curtain::to_text`, `Height`, `Width`), which `Importer::profiled_wall` reads back. The app (`wall_profile_ui.rs`): *Edit Wall Profile* and *Reset Wall Profile* (Modify tab and the wall palette's *Profile* section, `UiCommand::EditWallProfile`/`ResetWallProfile`) open the profile in `profile_sketch`'s canvas as `sketch_mode::Target::WallProfile` (lines and arcs; reference lines at the ends, base, top and nearby levels, hosted openings drawn faintly), finish with `sketch_set` and warn when hosted elements no longer fit (`misfits`). Per-layer extents (`cc_core::layer_extent`): each `Layer` of a wall type takes `base_offset` (from the wall's bottom at each point: base, profile chain or attached top) and `height: Option<f64>` (from its own bottom, clamped to the wall's top; `None` to the top), serde defaults skipped when default (bounds `MAX_OFFSET`, `MIN_HEIGHT`/`MAX_HEIGHT`; repair resets bad ones and clears floors' and roofs'; `type_set` checks them, `check_layer_extents`). `cc_geom::wall_layers` (a child of `wall`): `WallGeom::layer_extents` (empty when all are default: the plain path, bit for bit) groups layers into `Band`s of one extent; within a column every height is a `Lin` along the wall and `split_bands` (after `set_elevation`/`set_top_profile`) splits columns where any two cross, so `extent_lines(c, extent)` (decided at the column's middle; openings cut every band they cross) holds across it (not in a mitered end's stretch). `intervals_at`/`c.solid` are the bands' union (joins, rooms, bars; the join height filter reaches the lowest layer), `layer_intervals`/`intervals_at_v` one layer's, `banded_solid` steps tops, bottoms, the faces between bands and the caps per band, `layer_volumes` (strip area times heights at the strip's centroid, less recess shares; a banded wall's `volume` is their sum), `volumes_by_material`, `layer_range`, `layer_outlines` (elevation polygons, openings left in). Plans draw each band cut, seen below or not (`plan::draw_banded_wall`; T cleanup as if the layers stood at the cut); section cuts keep one cut material over the stepped outline. `element_get` `derived.layers` (`material`, `thickness`, `volume`, `base`, `top` above the base). IFC export writes one body item per layer in its material (`layer_bodies`: strips extruded, or profile outlines swept and every item clipped when one is) and `WALL_LAYERS_PSET` (`ConstructCAD_WallLayers`: `Extents` in the type's `HasPropertySets`, `Height`/`Width` on each wall), which `Importer::layered_wall`/`layer_extents` read back.
- **Spline walls** — first stage (#307): `cc_core::wall_spline`: `Wall::spline` (`Option<WallSpline { control }>`, format 32) is the wall's location line as the control points of a chain of cubic Béziers (the form of `cc_core::spline`; `WallSpline::through` interpolates fit points); the wall then has no bulge and `start`/`end` are the curve's ends (`EditCtx::finish` → `sync_wall_splines`: the curve wins; ends moved while the curve stayed take it along, whole when both moved alike, else each end's knot and handle, `move_end`). `WallSpline::path` flattens it into a `WallPath` (stations with the curve's true unit normals and signed curvature; chords at most `SAGITTA` 0.5 mm off the curve and `MAX_TURN` 6° apart, `MAX_CHORDS`, tolerances loosened past it; `u` is the distance along the chords): `at(u, v)` lies between the stations' points `v` along their normals, so a line at a constant `v` is the polyline through the stations' exact offsets, straight past the ends; `locate` is its exact inverse (a quadratic per chord); `offset`, `tangent`, `line`, `face_near`, `min_radius`, `face_radius`. `WallSpline::check(left, right)` (faces as signed offsets of the location line, `Project::wall_faces`; `Project::check_wall_spline`) gives the path or a `SplineError` (`Points`, `Short`: open and `MIN_LENGTH` long, `Corner`: a kink past `MAX_KINK_DEG` where segments join, `Tight`: the face inside a bend keeps less than `MIN_FACE_RADIUS`, `Crossing`: a face crosses itself or the other; `key()` i18n `wall-spline-*`, `point()`); `repair_wall_splines` (before the element loop) drops a curve that fails it or stands on a curtain type (the wall stays straight between its ends), puts the ends on the curve and clears bulge, profile and attached top, and the element loop removes doors, windows, niches, bar sets and framings hosted by a spline wall. `Wall::arc()` is `None` for one; `is_curved`, `spline_path`, `length`, `at`, `project`, `tangent_at` follow the curve; `Project::wall_path` is the body centerline (the location line's `offset`), `wall_axis` its ends. `Element::translate`/`transform` move the control points (a mirror reverses them); `wall_ops`: `is_spline`, `path_of` gives none and `path` `EditError::Spline` (trim, extend, align refuse: `msg-spline-wall`), `split` splits the curve (`WallSpline::split`), `offset_copy` offsets it (`spline::offset`, approximate; checked), `move_endpoint` works through the sync. `cc_geom`: `WallLine::path` and `WallGeom::spline` hold the centerline path (`Arc<WallPath>`; `is_curved`); `world`, `local`, `tangent`, `frame_at`, `path` take it, `WallGeom::steps` meshes a column at every station of either face (fractions; arcs and lines as before, bit for bit), `facet_normal` gives a spline's side facets their own normals (so `Solid::volume` is the chords' exactly), areas are the chords' polygons (`outline_area`, `strip_area`), faces are `Face::smooth`. Joins: a spline wall's face is `Curve::Poly`, its chords within `FACE_REACH` of the joint carried on straight past its ends, met exactly with lines, circles and other polylines, so its ends miter and butt like any wall's and walls end on its curved side (`face_gaps`, layer cleanup included); it never embeds or is embedded. Bands (layer extents) work as they are. Plans, projected views, rooms, tags, dimensions (`dim_refs` reads `is_curved`) and schedules come from those. No openings, recesses, attached tops, profiles, curtain grids, bars or framing on a spline wall (`build_walls` and `rebar` skip them). Commands (`cc_engine::commands::wall_spline`, helpers only): `wall_create` `through` (the points between `start` and `end`) or `control` (all of them, from `start` to `end`), `check_wall` (also from `element_set`, which patches `params.spline`), `refuse` in `hosted_at`, `wall_attach`, `wall_profile`, `wall_rebar_create`, `rebar_copy`, framing, `strip_footings_under_walls` (skipped), `element_align`; `element_get` `derived.spline { segments, min_radius, face_radius, knots }`. IFC export writes an `IfcWall` `NOTDEFINED` with a `Brep` body (the capped solid, faces wound outward) in the storey's frame, no axis and no layer set usage (the type carries the layer set); import brings it back as a component by the fallback. In the app the wall tool's *Spline* mode (`tools::WallMode`, `spline_wall_tool`) takes the points clicked (Enter, Esc through `tools::spline_wall_escape` as it ends a chain of straight walls, a click on the last point or a double-click makes the wall with `wall_create` `through`; Backspace takes a point back) over a preview checked by `Project::check_wall_spline` (faces from `wall_faces`; a failure is drawn in the warning color at `SplineError::point`, its `key()` shown and the wall refused), the Select tool gives a selected spline wall a grip per inner knot (`Drag::Knot`: `sketch_mode::spline_moved` keeps the handles, written through `engine_edit::element`) and previews its end grips on the curve, the door, window and niche tools answer `msg-spline-wall-hosts`, and the palette leaves out the arc angle and the profile section. Notes for the next stages: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-spline-walls-notes.md`.
- **Niches** — a niche is a hosted component like a door or a window that recesses one face of its wall instead of opening it: `ComponentKind::Niche` definitions whose `HostOpening::depth` is an expression (`openings::niche_def`: Width, Height, Depth and Sill parameters, no pieces; `ComponentDef::opening_depth`, `ElementType::opening_depth`) make types of `Category::Niche`, and `ElementKind::Niche(Hosted)` elements (`as_hosted` covers them, so they move, copy, phase and go with their wall like doors; `flip_facing` puts them in the wall's right face). `cc_geom::wall::Recess` (`WallGeom::recesses`, `set_recesses` after `set_openings`: kept inside the faces and height, no deeper than leaves `RECESS_MIN_BACK`, left out when it overlaps an opening or an earlier recess) does not split the wall into columns, so joins, rooms, bars and framing see the wall whole (`wall_framing` and the wall's net area skip niches); `to_solid` opens the face in pieces (`face_pieces`) and adds the recess's back, sides, bottom and top, `volume` subtracts `recess_volume` (exact along arcs), and `ModelGeometry::volume` of a niche is what it takes out. Plans (`plan::draw_recesses`) cover the wall's fill where a cut niche is and draw its sides and back, as primitives of the niche (hidden with its category), with a projection line along the face for its sill when the wall stands under it; one wholly below the cut is hidden, one wholly above hidden with a `LinePattern::Dash` pen (ceiling plans: both hidden), and `face_segments_at` opens the cut face; elevations pick a niche by an `OpeningSymbol` outline off its face. `cc_engine::commands::niche`: `niche_type_create`, `niche_create` (makes the default type in the same undo step when the project has none; new projects carry it, `Defaults::niche`, added last by `from_template_system`); `element_get` `derived` has `width`, `height`, `depth`, `face` and `volume`. IFC export writes an `IfcOpeningElement` `RECESS` with `NICHE_PSET` (`ConstructCAD_Niche`); import does not read it back yet. The app reuses `Tool::Hosted` (`Category::Niche`, *Architecture → Build → Niche*, `Icon::Niche`); its options bar has the sill of windows and niches placed next (`AppState::hosted_sill`, `tools::hosted_sill`; cleared by `set_tool` and a type change).
- **Wall flip** — `cc_core::wall_flip`: swaps a wall's exterior and interior sides with its location line kept in place. `Wall::reversed` walks the same line the other way (ends and `join_start`/`join_end` swapped, `bulge` negated, `WallSpline::reversed`, `WallProfile::reversed`), so the layers (listed from the left face) mirror about the location line: a wall on a finish or core line keeps that line and its body changes side. `EditCtx::flip_walls` (checked first by `Project::check_wall_flip`: `FlipError::TooTight` when an arc's face on the other side would pass its center, `FlipError::Spline` when the reversed spline fails `check_wall_spline` with the body on the other side; nothing changes then) also reverses along the wall what is measured on the body centerline: `CurtainEdits::remap` and hosted `offset`s (`reverse_along`: `L - u`, scaled by the new centerline length on an arc wall whose centerline moves), toggles `flip_facing` and `flip_hand` of its doors, windows and niches (a half turn of their frame: they stay put, facing the same way), moves its `WallHorizontal`/`WallVertical` bar sets to `WallSide::other` (the bars stay on the face that looks the same way in the world) and points dimension references at the same points (`Line`: `from_end` toggled, `across` negated; `Hosted` on its openings: both negated). What only the derived geometry knows the engine sets in the same edit (`modify::flip_keep`, before the flip): a vertical set's `offset` from `cc_geom::flipped_vertical_offset` (what is left after its last station before the cover at the face's end after joins, snapped to the nanometer, so its stations map to `L - u` and flipping twice gives the offset back), and, for a curtain wall laid out by its type's vertical grid whose lines counted from the other end differ (fixed distance justified at the start or the end; centered, fixed number and maximum spacing are symmetric), the wall's own `CurtainEdits::vertical` lines at the mapped positions (`Project::flipped_along`), so mullions stay put; the wall then no longer follows that type's vertical grid (the type is not changed). Paints are world-anchored and stay. Not kept in place: the studs of a wall framing, regenerated from the new start by `sync_member_hosts` (the framing has no setting to count from the end); ties, which follow every `every_vertical`-th station of the face now exterior from the new start (they stay only when both faces' stations match and the count fits); the lap stagger of spliced vertical bars, alternating by station index (it swaps when a set has an even number of bars); and anything along an arc wall whose centerline moves (its body moves anyway). Commands (`cc_engine::commands::modify`): `wall_flip { walls }` (one undo step, `tx-wall-flip`; pinned walls skipped, a wall that cannot flip fails the whole command) and `elements_turn` (Space on a selection), which flips the selected walls in the same step as it turns the rest, leaving out the doors and windows of a flipped wall (they go with it) and reporting `not_flipped`. The app: Space with the Select tool runs `elements_turn` (`tools::rotate_selection`, status `msg-walls-flipped` or `msg-rotated`), *Flip Walls* (`UiCommand::FlipWalls`, *Modify | Walls* and the right-click menu, shortcut shown as Space) runs `wall_flip` (`modify::flip_walls`), and in the wall tool Space toggles `Interaction::wall_flip` (`msg-wall-side-flipped`): the preview draws the body on the other side of the line (`center` negated) and `create_wall` makes the wall from the second point to the first (bulge negated; a spline's fit points reversed, `spline_fit`).
